# Support

Dreamy Photo is maintained and published by [Jiaranbb](https://github.com/Jiaranbb).

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Jiaranbb/dreamy-photo/issues) for reproducible bug reports, compatibility questions, and feature requests.

Include:

- the user request that triggered the Skill;
- the source-image category without attaching private media;
- the selected mood, aspect ratio, strength, primary mechanism, and support mechanism;
- what should have remained unchanged;
- what failed in the generated result;
- the Codex app and image-model version when available.

Do not post private photos, faces, credentials, local absolute paths, or unredacted conversation logs in a public issue. Use a redistributable or synthetic reproduction whenever possible.
